## Risk Notes

- OTC/CSE/microcap names may carry liquidity, dilution, promotional, and regulatory risk.
- Company press releases are company-source claims, not independently verified clinical evidence.
- Wellness, RUO, cosmetic, or commercial peptide products should not be treated as regulated drug assets without primary evidence.
- Market moves should be logged as context only unless paired with a public-source catalyst.
- This is not a buy/sell recommendation. Verify independently.
