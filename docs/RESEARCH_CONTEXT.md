# Reconciled Research Context

This is the single-source research context for Peptide Stock Tracker after integrating the original report, Kimi output, and Gemini output.

## Executive synthesis

The peptide ecosystem is shifting from a gray-market/RUO/compounding-adjacent space toward a more formal FDA-reviewed compounding and development environment. The central 2026 catalyst is the FDA Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee meeting scheduled for July 23–24, 2026. The committee will review several target peptides for potential inclusion on the 503A Bulks List.

This does not make the peptides FDA-approved drugs. It may affect compounding eligibility, enforcement discretion, pharmacy behavior, telehealth platform narratives, CDMO demand, and investor attention.

## Major analytical guardrails

1. PCAC review is not FDA approval.
2. Category 1 or Bulks List movement is not proof of safety or efficacy.
3. Telehealth/compounding infrastructure is not the same as asset ownership.
4. Press-release product claims are not clinical validation.
5. TB-500 fragment and full-length thymosin beta-4/timbetasin must remain separate.
6. GHK-Cu regulatory status must be route-specific.
7. Microcap/OTC claims require stronger review due to dilution, promotion, and liquidity risk.
8. Company-specific “beneficiary” language should be framed as `possible market relevance`, not investment advice.
