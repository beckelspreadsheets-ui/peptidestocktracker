# Compliance

This project is for public-source research and alerting only.

## Allowed
- Official public APIs and public pages.
- Company filings, press releases, investor pages, and exchange filings.
- Public patent databases.
- Public trial registries.
- Public FDA/Federal Register/Regulations.gov pages.
- PubMed/NCBI public APIs.

## Not allowed
- Material nonpublic information.
- Insider tips or rumors.
- Hacking, credential misuse, or private endpoints.
- CAPTCHA bypassing, paywall bypassing, or ToS-violating scraping.
- Buy/sell recommendations or price targets.
- Medical instructions for unapproved peptides.

## Alert language rule

Every alert must include: "This is not a buy/sell recommendation. Verify independently."

## Microcap risk language

Any OTC/CSE/microcap alert must mention liquidity, dilution, promotional, and regulatory risk when relevant.
